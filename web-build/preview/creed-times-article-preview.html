Key Points

  • The story is separated from commentary and background.
  • Important context is surfaced before the long read.
  • Sources and references remain visible at the end.

This preview shows the final reading direction for Creed Times 2.0. The article body uses generous spacing, a narrower reading measure and editorial typography so long-form stories remain comfortable on desktop and mobile.

Why the context matters

Instead of surrounding the reader with unnecessary sidebars and advertising-style blocks, the interface keeps attention on the story. Supporting elements—author details, related stories, sharing, notes and sources—remain easy to reach without competing with the text.

Logged-in users can save a story, add private notes, follow authors and return to reading history from their profile. Premium stories can display a Creed Pro gate while preserving the public headline, excerpt and metadata.

The goal is a newsroom interface that feels modern without making serious reading feel like a dashboard.

Sources and transparency

Editors can add key points, source links, audio, update labels and developing-story status from the WordPress publishing screen.
